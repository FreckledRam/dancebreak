Breaker rankings

Elo rating from every recorded 1 vs 1 battle. A breaker is ranked after 5 battles. Click a breaker for their history.

People are matched by name. Two breakers who share a name are counted as one; one breaker with two names is counted as two. Coverage before 2025 is incomplete, which affects ratings.

Judges

How each judge votes compared with the rest of the panel. Click a judge for the systems they have judged.

With majority: share of a judge's round votes that matched the panel's majority. Lone dissent: share where they were the only judge on their side. Votes for red: 50% would be even.

Judging systems

How each system behaves in practice across the battles that used it.

Judges and Rounds are averages per battle. Unanimous: rounds where every judge picked the same side. One-vote: rounds decided by a single judge. Red wins: share of decided battles won by the red side.
